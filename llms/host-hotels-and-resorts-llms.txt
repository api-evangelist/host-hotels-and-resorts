- [**History**](https://www.hosthotels.com/Our-Company/History):\
Host Hotels & Resorts, Inc. traces its origin to "The Hot Shoppe," a Washington, D.C. root beer stand opened in 1927 by J. Willard and Alice Marriott.\
The current corporate structure was established in 1993, when a tax-free spin-off of Marriott Corporation created **Host Marriott Corporation**, retaining lodging real estate. The company initially owned 24 full-service hotels, with a Total Enterprise Value (TEV) of $3.6 Billion. By 1996, the strategy focused on acquiring high-quality, full-service hotels.\
Host expanded rapidly, launching a brand diversification strategy in 1998 by acquiring world-class hotels from the Blackstone Group. Through major transactions, the company qualified as the **largest U.S. Lodging REIT** effective January 1, 1999. In 2006, a $3.5 billion acquisition of a Starwood portfolio established an international presence and diversified operators, leading to the name change to **Host Hotels & Resorts, Inc.**.\
Between 2012 and 2014, Host achieved **investment-grade status** for its debt and received recognition for sustainable real estate practices. In 2019, the company issued the lodging industry’s **first green bond**. Today, Host operates as the **Premier Lodging REIT**, owning 81 consolidated hotels in top U.S. markets with a TEV of $17.2 Billion as of December 31, 2024.

- [**Strategy**](https://www.hosthotels.com/Corporate-Responsibility/Strategy):
Host Hotels & Resorts’ core strategy is **Value Creation Through Responsible Investment**, centralizing its Corporate Responsibility program around this approach. Sustainable financing is a key component, integrating environmental initiatives into its acquisition and redevelopment pipelines, which also helps reduce the cost of capital.\
The company has secured nearly **$5 billion** in financing with sustainability attributes, including green bonds and a sustainability-linked credit facility with two-way pricing incentives. Host is the first lodging REIT to issue green bonds, aligning its framework with international principles.\
These investments generate significant returns: **863 sustainability projects** yield an expected **$24 million** in annual utility savings and achieve 13-20% average cash-on-cash returns. Green bond proceeds are specifically allocated to acquiring and renovating **LEED certified properties** (Silver and Gold) and funding development projects, such as the LEED Gold® retrofit of the corporate office in Bethesda, Maryland.
- [**2050 Vision**](https://www.hosthotels.com/Corporate-Responsibility/2050-Vision): Host Hotels & Resorts’ aspirational vision is to become a net positive company by 2050, with specific 2030 targets serving as interim milestones in this roadmap. This vision is categorized across five focus areas:
  1. Environmental Impact: The goal is to achieve a net positive environmental impact throughout the value chain. Key 2030 targets include a 54% absolute reduction of greenhouse gas emissions from a 2019 baseline (32.7% reduced as of 2024), sourcing 50% of electricity from renewable sources (25.2% as of 2024), and achieving a 25% reduction of water usage in water-stressed areas.
  2. Climate Resilient Portfolio: Host aspires to own one of the most resilient portfolios in the industry, targeting 40% of consolidated hotels to achieve LEED green building certification by 2030 (24.7% achieved as of 2024).
  3. Inclusive Workplace: The goal is to be an employer of choice and lead with a measurable culture of inclusion and belonging. Targets include maintaining 85% or more highly engaged employees annually (88% achieved in the most recent survey) and ensuring candidate slates for externally sourced positions are diverse.
  4. Community Impact: Host aims to be a catalyst for positive impact, targeting 90% or more employee participation in charitable giving and/or volunteerism each year (91% achieved as of 2024).
  5. Resilient Supply Chain: The company intends to build a highly responsible and resilient supply chain, with targets including 100% of direct suppliers trained annually on responsible sourcing and human rights policies
  This vision underscores the company’s commitment to creating long-term value by investing responsibly in its business, environment, people, and community
